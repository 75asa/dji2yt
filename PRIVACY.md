# Privacy Policy — dji2yt

dji2yt is a personal, self-hosted tool used only by its owner.

- It accesses the owner's Google Drive (to read and rename uploaded videos),
  YouTube (to upload videos and manage playlists) and Google Calendar
  (read-only, to classify videos by event title).
- OAuth tokens and processing state are stored only on the machine where the
  tool runs. No data is sent to any third party or to the developer.
- Access can be revoked at any time from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/75asa/dji2yt/issues
